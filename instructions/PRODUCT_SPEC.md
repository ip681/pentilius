# PENTILIUS — Product Specification

## Status labels
- **LOCKED** — agreed decision.
- **PROVISIONAL** — agreed direction, balance may change.
- **UNDEFINED** — not decided yet.

## Core concept — LOCKED
A meteorite strikes Earth and changes humanity. Over generations, survivors develop into five distinct human races/bloodlines.

The five races eventually understand that they need one another.

Humanity leaves Earth and colonizes the planet Pentilius.

Pentilius has an atmosphere unsuitable for all five human races equally. Humans use helmets/oxygen systems outside protected bases and process the local atmosphere to produce oxygen.

Earth crops initially grow only in controlled environments.

Pentilius contains dangerous native creatures called **Pentili**.

Deep within the planet exists an ancient system connected to maintaining the current atmosphere/ecosystem. It was not built for humans and is not a convenient terraforming machine.

Humans discover that they can influence this system through a massive human-built megastructure called **the Core**.

As the Core progresses toward activation, Pentili behavior changes: migrations increase, attacks intensify and stronger Pentili appear.

The first clan to complete, defend and successfully activate the Core wins the season.

A new season restarts the same competitive race.

## Audience — LOCKED
Pentilius is intended for busy working people.

- No manual character movement.
- No manual ship piloting.
- No action-combat requirement.
- Battles are automatically simulated.
- Short sessions should provide meaningful progress.
- Long automatic activities such as expeditions are desirable.

## Races — LOCKED at system level
There are exactly five human races.

They are not enemy factions.

Clans benefit from having players from multiple races.

Exact race names, visuals, lore details and final racial stat bonuses remain **UNDEFINED**.

## Racial group synergy — PROVISIONAL
For cooperative content:

- 1 unique race: 0%
- 2 unique races: +5%
- 3 unique races: +10%
- 4 unique races: +15%
- 5 unique races: +25%

Do not create boss-specific race roles. Races improve their own player stats/builds; group diversity provides synergy.

## Ship and base — LOCKED
There are no separate ship classes/types as the core progression model.

There are no separate base classes/types.

The player's ship is defined by the equipped parts/items and their upgrades.

The base is defined by its buildings and development.

## Seasons — LOCKED
The game is seasonal.

Successful activation of the Core ends the season.

The next season restarts the same race.

Exact season length and persistence rules are still **UNDEFINED**.

## Platforms — LOCKED
Initial platform: browser.

Future platform: mobile app.

The same backend must serve both.
